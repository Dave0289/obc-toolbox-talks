Housekeeping & Trip Hazards

Week of January 12, 2026 • Steel Shop / Yard
Toolbox Talk

Purpose

Good housekeeping prevents slips, trips, falls, and struck-by incidents in the shop and yard.

Common Hazards (Steel + Concrete)

  • Cutoffs, banding, welding leads, extension cords, hoses across walkways
  • Grinding dust, debris piles, rebar tie wire, loose anchors or form hardware
  • Oil leaks, wet spots, wash-down areas, slurry and mud at concrete operations
  • Blind corners, clutter near crane aisles, forklift travel lanes, laydown areas

Controls

  • Keep travel lanes marked and clear. Don’t store material in walkways.
  • Coil and hang leads/hoses. Use cord covers when crossing paths.
  • Clean spills immediately. Barricade if cleanup can’t happen right now.
  • End-of-task cleanup: leave the area better than you found it.

Stop Work / Speak Up

If you see a trip hazard that could injure someone, stop and correct it or notify your supervisor immediately.
