# Forklift & Pedestrian Interface  
**Week of:** January 26, 2026  
**Applies to:** Shop, Field, Yard  

---

## ENGLISH

### Purpose
To prevent struck-by and crushed-by injuries involving forklifts and other powered industrial trucks.

### Common Hazards (Steel & Concrete)
- Blind corners and congested travel lanes  
- Elevated loads blocking the operator’s view  
- Backing without a spotter  
- Pedestrians entering forklift operating zones  
- Unstable or improperly secured loads  

### Controls
- Keep forklift routes and pedestrian walkways clearly marked. Separate people and equipment whenever possible.  
- Sound the horn at intersections, doorways, and blind corners. Reduce speed in tight areas.  
- Travel with forks/load low and slightly tilted back for stability. Do not travel with elevated loads.  
- Use a spotter when backing, when the load blocks visibility, or when working in congested areas.  
- Never allow pedestrians near the load path or under elevated loads. Maintain safe clearance at all times.  
- Secure loads and keep them within the forklift’s rated capacity. If the load is unstable, re-stack or re-band it.  
- Park correctly: forks flat on the ground, controls neutral, and key removed when unattended.  

### Stop Work / Speak Up
If traffic patterns are unsafe or visibility is obstructed, stop operations and correct the condition before continuing.

## ESPAÑOL

### Propósito
Prevenir lesiones por golpes o aplastamientos causadas por montacargas.

### Peligros Comunes (Acero y Concreto)
- Esquinas ciegas y pasillos congestionados  
- Cargas elevadas que bloquean la visión  
- Retroceder sin señalero  
- Peatones en zonas de operación  
- Cargas inestables  

### Controles
- Mantenga rutas de montacargas y pasillos peatonales claramente marcados.  
- Toque la bocina en intersecciones y puntos ciegos. Reduzca la velocidad en áreas estrechas.  
- Transporte la carga baja y estable. No viaje con la carga elevada.  
- Use señalero cuando retroceda o cuando la visibilidad esté limitada.  
- Nunca permita personas cerca de la trayectoria de la carga.  
- Asegure las cargas dentro de la capacidad del montacargas.  
- Estacione con horquillas en el piso y equipo asegurado.  

### Detener el Trabajo / Hablar
Si el tránsito no es seguro o la visibilidad es deficiente, detenga la operación y corrija antes de continuar.
