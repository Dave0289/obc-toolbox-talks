# Grinding & Abrasive Wheel Safety  
**Week of:** January 26, 2026  
**Applies to:** Shop, Field, Yard  

---

## ENGLISH

### Purpose
To prevent eye injuries, lacerations, and wheel failures when using grinders and abrasive tools.

### Common Hazards (Steel & Concrete)
- Wheel breakage due to improper mounting  
- Sparks and flying metal  
- Missing guards and tool rests  
- Using damaged or expired wheels

### Controls
- Inspect wheels before use and perform ring test on new wheels.  
- Use guards and maintain proper tool rest clearance.  
- Wear face shield and eye protection.  
- Allow grinders to reach full speed before contacting material.  
- Never exceed rated wheel speed.  

### Stop Work / Speak Up
If a wheel is damaged or a guard is missing, stop and replace before using the tool.

---

## ESPAÑOL

### Propósito
Prevenir lesiones oculares y fallas de discos abrasivos.

### Peligros Comunes (Acero y Concreto)
- Discos mal montados  
- Chispas y fragmentos  
- Guardas faltantes  
- Uso de discos dañados

### Controles
- Inspeccione discos y realice prueba de sonido.  
- Use guardas y mantenga distancia correcta.  
- Use careta y lentes.  
- Permita que alcance velocidad completa.  
- No exceda la velocidad nominal.  

### Detener el Trabajo / Hablar
Si el disco está dañado o sin guarda, detenga el trabajo.
