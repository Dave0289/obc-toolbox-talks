# Steel Erection – Load Stability & Connections  
**Week of:** January 26, 2026  
**Applies to:** Shop, Field, Yard  

---

## ENGLISH

### Purpose
To prevent collapses and struck-by incidents during steel erection and connection work.

### Common Hazards (Steel & Concrete)
- Unstable members before final connections  
- Inadequate temporary bracing  
- Wind affecting suspended or set members  
- Pinch points during bolt-up and alignment  
- Working below partially secured steel

### Controls
- Install members in accordance with the erection plan and sequence.  
- Use required temporary bracing and guying.  
- Keep personnel clear of suspended and partially secured steel.  
- Use tag lines to control rotation.  
- Do not release the load until the required number of bolts or welds are in place and snug.  
- Monitor wind conditions and suspend lifts when limits are exceeded.  

### Stop Work / Speak Up
If steel is not stable or adequately secured, stop work and correct the condition before proceeding.

## ESPAÑOL

### Propósito
Prevenir colapsos y golpes durante el montaje de acero.

### Peligros Comunes (Acero y Concreto)
- Piezas inestables  
- Falta de arriostramiento temporal  
- Viento que afecta las cargas  
- Puntos de atrapamiento  
- Trabajar debajo de acero sin asegurar

### Controles
- Siga el plan y secuencia de montaje.  
- Use arriostramiento temporal.  
- Mantenga al personal fuera de cargas suspendidas.  
- Use cuerdas guía.  
- No libere la carga hasta que esté asegurada.  
- Detenga izajes con viento fuerte.  

### Detener el Trabajo / Hablar
Si el acero no está estable, detenga el trabajo y asegure antes de continuar.
