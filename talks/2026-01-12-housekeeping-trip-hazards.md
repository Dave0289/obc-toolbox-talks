# Housekeeping & Trip Hazards
**Week of:** January 12, 2026  
**Applies to:** Field & Shop  

## Purpose
Good housekeeping prevents slips, trips, falls, and struck-by incidents in both the fabrication shop and on the jobsite.

## Common Hazards (Steel & Concrete)
- Cutoffs, banding, welding leads, extension cords, and hoses across walkways
- Grinding dust, debris piles, rebar tie wire, loose anchors, and form hardware
- Oil leaks, wet spots, wash-down areas, slurry, and mud at concrete operations
- Blind corners, clutter near crane aisles, forklift travel lanes, and laydown areas

## Controls
- Keep travel lanes marked and clear. Do not store material in walkways.
- Coil and hang leads and hoses. Use cord covers when crossing paths.
- Clean spills immediately. Barricade the area if cleanup cannot be completed right away.
- Perform end-of-task cleanup — leave the area safer than you found it.

## Stop Work / Speak Up
If you see a trip hazard that could injure someone, stop work if necessary and correct it or notify your supervisor immediately.
